Upload students

Upload a sheet of students. Each row is registered on Skill India (NSDC) and comes back with a candidate ID.

  • Accepts .csv and .xlsx, up to 10 MB.
  • Required columns: namePrefix, name, dob, guardianName, email, phone, countryCode, Batch Name. Common spellings such as "Student Name", "Mobile" or "Course" are recognised automatically.
  • phone must be 10 digits and countryCode just the digits (91). A row where the two are the wrong way round is rejected — 512 candidates on NSDC have "91" as their phone number from a sheet where that happened.
  • Batch Name must read as <Programme> <Mon><YY> — Academy Sep26, DSML Jan27 — and match the batch exactly. The batch does not have to exist yet; one created afterwards fills itself in.
  • Gender is taken from namePrefix (Mr. → male, anything else → female), matching how NSDC records were created until now.
  • Preview payload only validates the sheet and shows the exact JSON that would be sent to NSDC — no request is made and no candidate is created.
  • Nothing is sent to NSDC until every row passes validation.
  • Already-registered students are not duplicated — their existing candidate ID is returned.
  • If the sheet names a batch per row — a Batch Name, Batch or Course column — the enrolment sheet for those students is built here, ready to upload on the Enrolment page. Students and batches can be uploaded in either order: a batch created afterwards fills itself in.
Dates must be written as YYYY-MM-DD 2003-07-15 for 15 July 2003. 15/7/2003 and 7/15/2003 are rejected, not guessed — the same date can be read two ways and a wrong date of birth cannot be corrected on NSDC afterwards.
