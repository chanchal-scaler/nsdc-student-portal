Generate certificates

NSDC issues certificates a training partner at a time, not a student at a time. One request covers everyone it considers eligible, and it takes no list — there is nothing here to fill in or choose. Who was certified is read back from NSDC afterwards, which is the second half of the run and the slower one.

Waiting on a certificate

  • The request carries no list. It names the training partner and nothing else — the account it is signed in as is the training partner, so the body is empty. NSDC decides who is eligible.
  • The students below are what this portal expects it to cover — results submitted here, no certificate recorded yet. It is what the run is measured against, not what it is told to do.
  • Students who already have a certificate are left alone by NSDC, so running this again costs a request and changes nothing.
  • Then NSDC is read back to see who ended up with one, because the request does not say. That read pages the whole candidate list, so it takes a few minutes on a large partner.

    Fetch the certificates themselves

    Generating a certificate and having a copy of it are two calls. NSDC hands them over one candidate at a time, so this fetches each in turn and puts them in a single zip — a few minutes for a large partner. A run stopped part way keeps what it fetched: starting it again asks only for the rest, and the zip comes out whole either way.
