Mark batch completed

Upload a sheet of students, the batch they finished, and whether they passed. Each batch's results are submitted to Skill India (NSDC), which completes the batch and issues certificates.

  • Accepts .csv and .xlsx, up to 10 MB. Three columns: email, batchName and result.
  • result takes 1 or 0, as the existing sheet does; Pass/Fail and true/false also work.
  • A pass is sent as 90% attendance, 90% assessment, grade A and certified; a fail as 50%, 50%, grade D and not certified. These are the fixed figures the existing script has always sent — the sheet carries no marks of its own.
  • Results are only sent for a student the portal has recorded as enrolled in that batch.
  • Students whose results were already submitted are skipped, so the same sheet can be run again safely.
  • Preview payload only shows the exact JSON that would be sent — no request is made and no batch is completed.
